## TIPS FOR SENDING:

### Best Practices:
1. **Choose the email version** that matches your manager's style:
   - Technical + Sales focused → Use Version 1
   - Loves details → Use Version 2
   - Very sales-driven → Use Version 3

2. **Timing:**
   - Send Tuesday-Thursday morning (9-11 AM)
   - Avoid Monday mornings or Friday afternoons
   - Don't send late at night

3. **Attachments:**
   - Attach all 4 documents
   - Name them clearly:
     - `Neoverse_Financial_Division_Executive_Summary.pdf`
     - `Neoverse_Financial_Division_Full_Proposal.pdf`
     - `Neoverse_Financial_Division_Financial_Model.pdf`
     - `Neoverse_Financial_Division_Service_Catalog.pdf`

4. **Follow-up:**
   - If no response in 3-4 days → Send follow-up email
   - After 1 week → Request 15-min meeting
   - Stay professional and patient

5. **Be Ready:**
   - Have answers ready for tough questions
   - Know your numbers cold
   - Be prepared to present in person
