Market Opportunity & Timing

1. Immediate Catalyst: SMS OTP Elimination (March 2026)

The UAE Central Bank has mandated that all financial institutions eliminate SMS-based one-time passwords (OTP) by March 2026 due to security vulnerabilities and increasing fraud incidents.

Impact: Every bank, exchange house, payment service provider, and insurance company in the UAE must implement alternative multi-factor authentication (MFA) solutions within the next 90 days.
