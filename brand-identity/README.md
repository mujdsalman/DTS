## 🎨 Quick Reference

### Color Palette

#### Primary Colors
- **Deep Blue**: `#0A2463` - Trust & Financial Credibility
- **Tech Purple**: `#6B4CE6` - Innovation & Technology

#### Secondary Colors
- **Vibrant Cyan**: `#00D9FF` - Digital Transformation
- **Electric Green**: `#00FF88` - Growth & Solutions

#### Neutral Colors
- **Charcoal**: `#1E1E1E` - Text & Elements
- **Light Gray**: `#F5F7FA` - Backgrounds
- **White**: `#FFFFFF`

#### Gradients
- **Purple to Cyan**: `linear-gradient(90deg, #6B4CE6, #00D9FF)`
- **Blue to Green**: `linear-gradient(90deg, #0A2463, #00FF88)`

### Typography

#### English
- **Headings**: Poppins (Bold, SemiBold, Medium)
- **Body**: Inter (Regular, Medium)

#### Arabic
- **All Text**: IBM Plex Arabic (Bold, Medium, Regular)

### Logo Usage

**Minimum Sizes:**
- Print: 30mm wide
- Digital: 120px wide

**Clear Space:**
- Minimum 1x symbol height on all sides

**Do:**
- ✓ Use provided logo files only
- ✓ Maintain proper clear space
- ✓ Use appropriate version for background

**Don't:**
- ✗ Stretch or distort
- ✗ Change colors
- ✗ Add effects or shadows
- ✗ Use below minimum size
