  • Expertise - Leveraging 12+ years of industry knowledge

Logo Usage Guidelines

✓ DO

  • Always maintain proper clear space (minimum 1x symbol height)
  • Use provided logo files only
  • Ensure logo is clearly legible at all sizes
  • Use appropriate version for background color

✗ DON'T

  • Don't stretch, distort, or rotate the logo
  • Don't change logo colors or add effects
  • Don't use logo smaller than 30mm wide (print) or 120px wide (digital)
  • Don't recreate or modify the logo
